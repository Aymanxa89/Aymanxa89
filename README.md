### 📂 Featured Engineering Projects

#### 1. [Crop Health Autonomous Rover (Agricultural Robot)](https://github.com/Aymanxa89/crop-health-rover)
> **Autonomous 4WD field rover deploying edge computer vision and long-range telemetry for real-time crop disease diagnosis.**
* **Edge AI:** Benchmarked YOLO11n vs. NanoDet-Plus on the PlantVillage dataset (54k images, 38 pathologies). Quantized to **NCNN INT8** for real-time inference on Raspberry Pi 4 CPU.
* **Electrical Architecture:** Isolated motor inductive spikes from logic rails using dual-battery decoupling and DC-DC buck regulators with LC filtering.
* **Telemetry & Sensing:** Multi-angle servo gimbal camera, environmental sensors, and **LoRa RF transmitter** for remote farm monitoring.
* `Raspberry Pi 4` `YOLO11n` `NCNN INT8` `LoRa` `L298N/TB6612FNG` `C/C++` `Python`

---

#### 2. [CanSat / CubeSat Avionics Telemetry Module](https://github.com/Aymanxa89/cansat-telemetry-avionics)
> **Simulated satellite telemetry payload with localized RF downlink and multi-layer Altium PCB design.**
* Engineered low-power telemetry using **ESP32** and **LoRa radio frequency** transceivers.
* Designed multi-layer PCB routing and custom component footprints in **Altium Designer** meeting aerospace vibration and EMC constraints.
* Built CRC-checked packet transmission routines for temperature, pressure, and altitude telemetry.
* `ESP32` `LoRa RF` `Altium Designer` `Sensors` `Embedded C` `Aerospace`

---

#### 3. [Custom Microcontroller Development Board & 5V Regulated Power Supply](https://github.com/Aymanxa89/custom-esp32-mcu-pcb)
> **Production-ready custom ESP32/Arduino development board and low-noise power supply engineered in Altium Designer.**
* Complete end-to-end PCB workflow: schematic capture, component footprint generation, high-density trace routing, and Gerber verification.
* Built a dedicated 5V regulated power supply with reverse-polarity protection, LC decoupling, and ground-pour thermal dissipation.
* `Altium Designer` `Hardware Design` `Power Electronics` `LTspice` `Gerber`

---

#### 4. [IoT High-Voltage Solar Panel Telemetry System](https://github.com/Aymanxa89/iot-solar-monitoring)
> **Real-time solar panel performance logger monitoring up to 24V / 5A with automated local database streaming.**
* High-precision current and voltage sensor interfacing with ESP32 ADC.
* Embedded C/C++ firmware with noise-filtering algorithms to track solar panel IV curve efficiency over WiFi.
* `ESP32` `C/C++` `ADC Filtering` `WiFi Telemetry` `IoT`
